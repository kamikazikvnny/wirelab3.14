PV Electrical Formulas

ARRAY POWER P = Panel W × Panels

Determines the total rated DC power of the PV array.

POWER P = E × I

Electrical power equals voltage multiplied by current.

CURRENT I = P ÷ E

Current can be calculated when power and voltage are known.

ENERGY kWh = kW × Hours

Estimates electrical energy produced over time.

FIELD KNOWLEDGE

Things Electricians Should Watch

☀ Solar Radiation

Increased solar irradiance generally increases PV current. Shading can significantly reduce array output.

🌡 Temperature

PV module voltage generally decreases as module temperature increases. Cold temperatures can produce higher operating voltages.

⚡ Voltage

PV strings must be evaluated for maximum voltage under the expected environmental conditions before equipment is selected.

🔧 Installation

Conductor sizing, overcurrent protection, disconnects, grounding, equipment ratings, and code requirements must be evaluated for the actual installation.
